Who We Are

Built on imagination.
Driven by stories.

Black Mountain Entertainment is an independently operated South African entertainment company developing original intellectual property and producing compelling screen experiences. As an independent production company, we develop our own projects and collaborate with creative talent and production partners.

Our ambition is to build stories that travel beyond the screen — from films and series to animation, visual effects, comics and original fictional worlds. We are open to working with broadcasters, streaming platforms, distributors, commissioners and other industry partners on suitable projects.
